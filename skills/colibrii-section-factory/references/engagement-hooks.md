# Engagement Hooks — Pattern Library

## Opening
- **Cold-open stat:** one devastating number, full-bleed, animated on first paint (`AN` counter). The reader must feel the gap before reading a paragraph.
- **BLUF block:** 3 sentences max, visually distinct (KeyInsight idiom), screenshot-ready.

## Scroll mechanics
- **Scroll-triggered reveals:** `ScrollReveal` / whileInView on every act — content earns its entrance.
- **Animated counters on entry:** numbers count up when scrolled into view (once).
- **Act-boundary tease:** each act ends with tension (open question, contrast, unresolved number) pulling to the next.
- **Scroll-progress indicator:** thin accent bar at viewport top within flagship sections.

## Interaction-first sections
- At least one act where the reader manipulates the model (sliders, comparators, scenario toggles) — defaults must already tell a story; interaction deepens it.
- Horizontal-scroll card rails for comparative cases: next card peeks (~15%) to signal scrollability; snap points; works with keyboard.

## Anchoring
- **"¿Qué significa para Costa Rica?"** every 2–3 scrolls — an explicit CR-implication beat (visually consistent callout).
- Local entities by name (ICE, ARESEP, MINAE, Intel CR) — recognition is retention.

## Growth loop
- **ShareBtn on every chart worth screenshotting** — exports branded PNG (logo watermark + source line) sized for LinkedIn/press.
- Hero stat and BLUF designed to survive a crop: self-contained, attributed, legible at 600px.
- Section deep-linkable (`#energia` hash) — shares land on the section, not the homepage.

## Restraint
No autoplay video, no scroll-jacking, no modal interrupts, no exit-intent. The hook is the analysis.
