# Narrative Voice — Insumo Técnico Independiente

## Register
Natural, direct Spanish. Short declarative sentences. Analytical layering: claim → evidence → implication. English mirrors the Spanish (toggle), never the reverse.

## The frame
Colibrii is an **independent technical input** to public debate. The data speaks; Colibrii arranges it. No partisan position, no policy cheerleading, no doom. **Never claim or imply Colibrii serves government** — we inform anyone who reads.

## Banned
- AI-sounding filler: "en un mundo cada vez más…", "es importante destacar que…", "sin duda", "juega un papel crucial"
- Motivational closers: "el futuro está en nuestras manos", "juntos podemos"
- Empty intensifiers: "increíble", "impresionante", "game-changer"
- Hedging stacks: "podría potencialmente llegar a"
- Partisan verbs for political actors: nobody "se niega tercamente" — they "votaron en contra (27-24)"

## Required moves
- **BLUF:** the hero states the 3 load-bearing facts in 3 sentences. A reader who stops there still leaves informed.
- **CR anchor:** every 2–3 scrolls, an explicit "¿qué significa esto para Costa Rica?" beat.
- **Numbers carry the drama:** "415 TWh → 945 TWh en seis años" needs no adjectives.
- **Attribution voice:** "según el ICE", "Moody's subió", "ARESEP fijó" — institutions act, the narrator doesn't editorialize.
- **Both sides as they'd say it:** stakeholder positions rendered so each actor would recognize their own argument.
- **Trade-offs, not verdicts:** recommendations are options with costs, addressed to the debate, not to a party.

## Sentence hygiene
≤25 words preferred. One idea per sentence. Active voice. Technical terms defined inline once (first use), then used without apology.
